Budget Bank Sync

Link your bank once. Budget Builder and Claude read your balances and transactions through your private connector.

Unlock

Enter your bridge key (the BRIDGE_SECRET you set in Vercel). It stays in this tab only.

Linked banks

Link Chase first. Each bank login brings in all its accounts, including credit cards. Plaid's free tier allows 10 bank logins.

Connect it to Claude

  1. In Claude, go to Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name: Budget Bank Sync (exactly, so Budget Builder can find it).
  3. URL:
  4. Authentication: No sign in. Under Request headers, add Authorization with the value Bearer, a space, then your bridge key.
